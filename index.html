ABOUT ME

About Me

I am a Computer Technology graduate from Politeknik Pajajaran ICB Bandung with an interest in Frontend Development and Internet of Things (IoT). During my studies, I learned the fundamentals of programming, web development, computer systems, and microcontroller technologies such as Arduino. These experiences helped me build a strong interest in technology and encouraged me to continue improving my technical skills through practical projects.

I enjoy learning by building real projects rather than only studying theory. Working directly with code, interfaces, hardware, and different components allows me to understand how a system works from the development process to the final result. I also enjoy finding solutions when I encounter problems during development because every problem gives me an opportunity to learn something new and improve my way of working.

In Frontend Development, I have experience building websites using HTML and CSS while continuously improving my JavaScript skills. I am interested in creating interfaces that are responsive, clean, user-friendly, and comfortable to access across different screen sizes. One of my projects is MochiCute, a dessert-themed website that I developed to improve my frontend development, visual design, and responsive web development skills.

I am also interested in modern frontend technologies and continue learning JavaScript, React, and other tools that can help me build better web applications. My goal is to become a Frontend Developer who can turn ideas and designs into functional and responsive websites while continuing to improve my understanding of development practices.

Besides frontend development, I also have an interest in Internet of Things and microcontroller-based systems. For my final project, I developed an Arduino-based vending machine control system using an Arduino Uno, coin acceptor, LCD, push buttons, and servo motors. Through this project, I gained hands-on experience in circuit design, Arduino programming, input processing, component integration, and troubleshooting a working electronic system.

My experience with Arduino and IoT also taught me that software and hardware can work together to create practical solutions. I enjoy experimenting with microcontrollers, sensors, and simple automation systems, and I want to continue developing projects that combine programming with real-world applications.

I understand that my skills are continuously developing and there are still many technologies that I want to explore. I enjoy learning through real projects, understanding problems, finding solutions, and improving my work step by step. Currently, I am focused on strengthening my Frontend Development skills, especially JavaScript and modern frontend technologies, while continuing to develop my knowledge of IoT and microcontroller systems.
